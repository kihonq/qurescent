Qurescent treats the Quran as a **fixed corpus with named pipelines**: glyph
fonts, translation editions, and guide pages that mark what is settled vs
traditionally reported.

The shell is Starlight (sidebar, search, theme). Learning notes live here; the
interactive mushaf is under [Mushaf](/read/).

## Sections

- [Mushaf](/read/) — surah index and verse-by-verse reading
- [Tajwid](/guide/tajwid/) — how color rules map onto QCF V4
- [Tadabbur](/guide/tadabbur/) — short reflections (not a tafsir corpus)
- [Lineage & history](/guide/lineage/) — nasab and revelation chronology with certainty styles

Verse text and tajwid are **zero-tolerance**. Anything that touches ayah text,
meaning, or color rules is checked against authoritative sources before it
ships.
