Qurescent is a **documentation-style manual for the Quran**. The site shell is
Starlight (sidebar, search, dark mode); the learning content lives under this
Guide, and the interactive mushaf is linked from the sidebar.

## Sections

- [Mushaf](/read/) — surah index and verse-by-verse reading
- [Tajwid](/guide/tajwid/) — how the color rules work, including the color legend
- [Tadabbur](/guide/tadabbur/) — reflections for contemplation (not a tafsir corpus)
- [Lineage & history](/guide/lineage/) — diagrams of prophetic lineage and revelation chronology

The Quran text and tajwid rendering are treated as zero-tolerance for error.
Anything that touches verse text, meaning, or tajwid is checked against
authoritative sources before it ships.
