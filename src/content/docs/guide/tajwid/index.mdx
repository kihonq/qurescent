The reader renders the Madani mushaf with **QCF V4 tajweed fonts** — tajwid
colors are baked into the glyphs themselves (the same approach Quran.com uses),
not painted on with CSS after the fact.

## What you’ll find here

- [Color legend](/guide/tajwid/color-legend/) — maps each visible color to its
  tajwid rule, validated against the Dar Al-Maarifah / Al-Azhar-endorsed legend

Word-by-word English glosses sit under each word and are independent of the
full-verse translation stack (English + Bahasa Melayu by default; change them
with **Aa Translations** in the chapter header).
