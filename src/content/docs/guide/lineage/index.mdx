distinguish **confirmed** vs **traditionally reported** material.

## What is safe to draw as confirmed

- Muhammad ﷺ → Adnan (22 links) — consensus, recorded in Sahih al-Bukhari
- The 23-year Meccan / Medinan revelation period

## What must be caveated

- Anything earlier than Adnan (toward Ismaʿil / Ibrahim) — classical reports
  disagree on intervening ancestors (variants of 7 / 9 / 15 / 40)
- Exact first-revelation date and precise surah-by-surah chronological order
