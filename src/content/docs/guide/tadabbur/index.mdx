Tadabbur pages here are short reflections meant to support contemplation. They
may cite tafsir inline where useful, but Qurescent does **not** ship a
standalone tafsir corpus.

Content granularity (per-ayah vs per-surah vs thematic) is still open — this
landing page is a placeholder until the first reflections are authored.
