Tadabbur here means short reflections for contemplation. Inline tafsir citations
are fine when they help; Qurescent does **not** ship a standalone tafsir corpus.

Granularity (per-ayah vs per-surah vs thematic) is still open — this page is a
stub until the first reflections are authored.
